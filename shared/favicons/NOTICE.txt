Site icons identify their respective websites in mica's shortcuts, history and tabs.
The artwork and trademarks belong to their respective owners. No affiliation or endorsement is implied.

Retrieved 2026-11-11 from DuckDuckGo's public favicon cache and the websites' public assets. Exact source URLs and host aliases are recorded in catalog.json.
Images are normalized to 64 px PNG, with their original colors and proportions preserved.

The small bundled catalog is an offline fallback. Icons reported by a visited page replace it.
For other public hosts, mica tries the website directly and then https://icons.duckduckgo.com/ip3/<host>.ico.
Only the hostname is sent to that service, without page paths, queries, cookies or a Referer.
Local names, IP addresses and nonstandard ports are excluded from the remote fallback.
